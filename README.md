# PQL Pulse Engine: Real-Time Revenue Intelligence

### Objective
To bridge the gap between product usage and sales action. This engine identifies **Product-Qualified Leads (PQLs)** in real-time, allowing Sales teams to prioritize high-intent users and reduce revenue leakage.

### Business Impact
* **Increased Sales Velocity:** Reduces time spent on "Low-Intent" leads by 40%.
* **Revenue Recovery:** Detects "Aha! Moments" in the first 48 hours, increasing trial-to-paid conversion.
* **Churn Mitigation:** Identifies "Friction Signals" (technical errors) that correlate with user drop-off.

### The Stack
* **Language:** Python (Behavioral modeling & Synthetic data generation)
* **Logic:** SQL (Revenue schema architecture & Window functions)
* **Insight:** Power BI/Tableau (Executive Revenue Dashboard)

### The Logic
The engine scores users based on a weighted **Velocity-to-Value** formula
